## Understanding Contact Limits vs. Email Send Limits

Before comparing software options, it is critical to distinguish between two distinct metrics used in free email plans:

1. **Subscriber / Contact Storage Limit:** The total number of unique email addresses you can store in your account database simultaneously.
2. **Email Send Limit (Monthly or Daily):** The total number of individual email messages you are permitted to send over a specific timeframe.

A high contact storage cap does not automatically mean high sending volume. For example, a platform may let you store thousands of contacts for free but restrict daily email output to a few hundred messages. Knowing whether your primary bottleneck is contact growth or sending frequency will dictate which free software fits your workflow best.

## 1. MailerLite

[MailerLite](https://www.mailerlite.com) is popular among small online businesses, solopreneurs, and newsletter publishers due to its clean, logical interface and intuitive design workflow.

MailerLite updated its Free plan structure in June 2026. The current Free plan provides entry-level publishing capabilities for small lists without requiring an immediate paid subscription.

### MailerLite Free Plan Specifications
* **Subscriber Limit:** Up to 250 active subscribers.
* **Monthly Send Limit:** Up to 2,500 emails per month.
* **Automation:** Includes up to 3 visual automation workflows (such as simple welcome series or lead magnet delivery).
* **Included Features:** Drag-and-drop email builder, rich-text editor, 3 signup forms/popups, 1 landing page, 1 website (up to 5 pages), 1 digital product or booking setup, 2 user seats, and a 14-day trial of premium features upon sign-up.
* **Account Verification:** Requires an account approval review before initial campaign sending to maintain deliverability standards across their network.

### Who It Is Suitable For
MailerLite’s Free plan is a practical choice for small businesses and solopreneurs who want to publish clean newsletters, create a basic opt-in landing page, and set up simple automated welcome messages for an initial list under 250 subscribers.

### Main Limitation
The primary constraint is the 250 active subscriber cap and 2,500 monthly email limit. Once your list exceeds 250 contacts, upgrading to a paid tier (**Comfort** or **Power**) is required to continue adding subscribers.

## 2. GetResponse

[GetResponse](https://www.getresponse.com) functions as an all-in-one marketing platform, combining email delivery with opt-in landing pages, sales funnels, and autoresponders.

GetResponse offers a Free plan designed for users launching new lead generation projects, alongside access to premium features during the initial registration period.

### GetResponse Free Plan Specifications
* **Contact Limit:** Up to 500 active contacts.
* **Monthly Send Limit:** Up to 2,500 newsletters per month.
* **Included Features:** Drag-and-drop email builder, 1 opt-in landing page, signup and contact forms, and basic email marketing tools.
* **14-Day Premium Access:** New registrations receive 14 days of access to premium tools (such as advanced marketing automations, sales funnels, and webinars). After the 14-day period, the account automatically remains on the Free plan tier unless the user chooses to upgrade to a paid plan (**Starter**, **Marketer**, or **Creator**).

### Who It Is Suitable For
GetResponse’s Free plan suits small businesses and entrepreneurs who need a 500-contact threshold and want to build a dedicated opt-in landing page to collect leads before investing in paid marketing tools.

### Main Limitation
After the initial 14-day premium feature access ends, advanced multi-branch automation workflows, webinar hosting, and e-commerce conversion funnels require upgrading to a paid plan.

## 3. Brevo

[Brevo](https://www.brevo.com) (formerly Sendinblue) takes a different architectural approach to pricing compared to most email platforms. Its free tier focuses on contact database capacity rather than subscriber volume caps.

### Brevo Free Plan Specifications
* **Contact Storage Limit:** Up to 100,000 contacts stored in your database for free.
* **Daily Email Send Limit:** Capped at 300 emails per day (allowing up to ~9,000 total email sends per month if distributed daily).
* **Automation Limit:** Marketing automation workflows can target up to 2,000 contacts on the free plan.
* **Included Features:** Drag-and-drop email editor, mobile-friendly templates, basic analytics reporting, email and SMS campaign tools, and customizable signup forms.

### Contact Storage vs. Sending Capacity Clarification
It is essential not to confuse Brevo's 100,000 contact storage limit with unlimited sending. While you can import and store 100,000 contacts without paying, you can only send **300 emails per day**. The 300-email daily limit means you cannot send a single campaign to 5,000 contacts on the Free plan in one day.

### Who It Is Suitable For
Brevo suits small businesses that maintain a large contact database (such as client rosters or inactive leads) but only send small, targeted daily batches, transactional notifications, or low-volume localized updates.

### Main Limitation
The strict 300 email/day send limit makes mass broadcast newsletters to subscriber lists larger than 300 people impractical without upgrading.

## 4. Kit (formerly ConvertKit)

[Kit](https://kit.com) was created specifically for bloggers, newsletter publishers, podcasters, and digital creators. Its platform emphasizes audience growth, tagging, and direct monetization.

Kit offers a high-capacity Free plan designed to help creators build an audience before monetizing their content.

### Kit Free Plan Specifications
* **Subscriber Limit:** Up to 10,000 subscribers.
* **Email Send Limit:** Unlimited email broadcasts to your subscriber list.
* **Forms & Landing Pages:** Unlimited opt-in forms and standalone landing pages.
* **Included Features:** Subscriber tagging and audience segmentation, digital product and subscription checkout, broadcast A/B testing, API access, and participation in the Kit Recommendations network.
* **Automation Limitation:** **Visual Automations** and automated **Email Sequences** (drip campaigns) are **not included** in Kit's Free plan. These feature sets require upgrading to a paid tier (**Newsletter** or **Creator**).

### Who It Is Suitable For
Kit’s Free plan is tailored for content creators, newsletter writers, and solopreneurs who send regular broadcast updates, build landing pages, and sell digital downloads to a growing audience (up to 10,000 subscribers) without needing automated email sequences on day one.

### Main Limitation
The primary trade-off is that Automated Email Sequences and Visual Automations are not included on the Free plan.

## 5. Mailchimp

[Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com) is one of the most recognized brand names in email marketing software. It provides an extensive template library and analytics reporting dashboards.

Mailchimp offers an entry-level Free plan designed for very small contact lists and initial software testing.

### Mailchimp Free Plan Specifications
* **Contact Limit:** Up to 250 total contacts across your account.
* **Monthly Send Limit:** 500 email sends per month.
* **Daily Send Limit:** Maximum 250 sends per day.
* **Account Limits:** 1 user seat and 1 audience list.
* **Sending Pauses:** If your stored contacts exceed 250 or your monthly sends exceed 500, sending capabilities are automatically paused until you reduce your list size or upgrade to a paid subscription.

### Who It Is Suitable For
Mailchimp’s Free plan is suitable for very small local projects, non-profit groups, or personal portfolios that maintain a tiny contact list (under 250 people) and only send one or two short updates per month.

### Main Limitation
The combination of a 250-contact cap and a strict 500 monthly send limit means that if you have 250 subscribers, you can only send **two emails per month** before reaching your send ceiling.

## How to Choose a Free Email Marketing Tool

Selecting the right free tool for your business requires evaluating your operational priorities against each platform's constraints. Consider these key decision factors:

### 1. Contact Storage vs. Monthly Send Volume
Evaluate whether your list growth or sending frequency is your primary constraint:
* If you have a **large contact list** but send infrequent updates, **Brevo** provides high storage capacity (100,000 contacts) with a 300 daily send cap.
* If you have a **growing newsletter list** and send frequent broadcasts, **Kit** allows up to 10,000 subscribers with unlimited broadcasts (without automation sequences).

### 2. Automation Requirements
Decide whether automated welcome emails or drip sequences are essential immediately:
* If you need **visual drip sequences** for lead magnet delivery on a small list, **MailerLite** includes 3 visual automations on its free plan (up to 250 subscribers).
* If you only need **manual broadcasts**, **Kit** provides high subscriber capacity without sequence tools.

### 3. Landing Pages and Form Builders
If you do not yet have a full website, built-in landing page builders allow you to collect leads immediately:
* **Kit** offers unlimited forms and landing pages.
* **MailerLite** includes 1 landing page, 1 website (up to 5 pages), and 3 opt-in forms.
* **GetResponse** includes 1 landing page and opt-in forms.

### 4. Direct Digital Monetization
If you plan to sell e-books, paid newsletters, or digital downloads directly from your email platform:
* **MailerLite** includes 1 digital product or booking setup on its free plan.
* **Kit** includes digital product and subscription checkout capabilities on its free plan.

### 5. Upgrade Path & Cost Scalability
Consider long-term pricing when your business outgrows free plan thresholds. Reviewing base paid tier entry points ensures you are comfortable with software costs as your subscriber base expands.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use email marketing for free?
Yes. Many email marketing software providers offer forever-free plans that allow you to store contacts, build opt-in forms, design newsletters, and send campaigns without paying a subscription fee, subject to contact and monthly send caps.

### What is the best free email marketing tool for a small business?
The best tool depends on your primary business needs. MailerLite is a good fit for small businesses needing simple automations and clean design (up to 250 subscribers). Kit is best for newsletter creators wanting high subscriber capacity (up to 10,000 subscribers) for broadcasts. Brevo is best for businesses storing large contact databases (up to 100,000 contacts) with low daily send requirements.

### What is the difference between contacts and email sends?
Contacts (or subscribers) refer to the total number of unique email addresses stored in your account database. Email sends refer to the total number of messages delivered. For example, sending one newsletter to 200 subscribers counts as 200 email sends.

### Which free email marketing tool allows the most subscribers?
Among major platforms, **Brevo** allows the highest contact storage (up to 100,000 contacts, subject to a 300 email/day send cap). **Kit** allows the highest broadcast subscriber capacity (up to 10,000 subscribers with unlimited broadcast emails).

### Which free email marketing tool is best for newsletters?
Kit is a strong fit for broadcast newsletters when you want a high subscriber allowance and unlimited broadcasts. MailerLite is a strong fit for visual newsletters when you want drag-and-drop design blocks, landing pages, and automated welcome workflows.

### Is free email marketing software enough for a small business?
Free email plans are usually sufficient when launching a business, testing an audience, or building an initial subscriber base. As your list grows beyond free contact thresholds or requires advanced sales funnels, CRM integrations, and dedicated support, upgrading to a paid plan becomes necessary.
